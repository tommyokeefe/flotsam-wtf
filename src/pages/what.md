# FLOTSAM (noun)
from [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/flotsam)

## 1 : floating wreckage of a ship or its cargo
broadly : floating debris
> flotsam washed up by the tide

## 2 :
### a : a floating population (as of emigrants or castaways)
> human flotsam

### b : miscellaneous or unimportant material
> a notebook filled with flotsam and jetsam

### C : debris, remains
> … the village … built on the flotsam of war. — Stan Sesser
